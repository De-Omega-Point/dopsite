# De-Omega-Point public website brand

Use the [main homepage](https://de-omega-point.com/) as the reference for company identity. Write **De-Omega-Point**; lead with the work and the governing principle: technology should serve people and keep them in control. Use Australian English and keep release claims evidence-based. Simplicity and quality go together.

- Use the approved company emblem at `/assets/de-omega-point-emblem-v2.png` without redrawing, cropping or stretching it, with a clear link home. Section and project logos may keep their own identities.
- Use the homepage's near-black foundation, light type and green accent for the parent brand. Reserve project palettes for their own content or tools. Do not force a site header into a game or duplicate a publication's masthead.
- The public **Portal** is `https://de-omega-point.com/apps/`, for apps and digital products. Keep old links and bookmarks working. The browse-only Portal is not the Commerce Hub, checkout or account area.
- Make navigation and focus states clear on touch and keyboard. Check the page at desktop and phone widths and preserve existing routes, accessibility and local browser data.
- Keep source and exported copies in sync (including `public/apps`, `public/bridging-light`, `public/lightbridge` and the De4Humanity source). The AI Safety publication is generated separately; change its shared template rather than hand-editing archive editions.
