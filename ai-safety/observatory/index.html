Protection centre

Practical safeguards for people and organisations. Guidance is general and does not replace incident-specific expert advice.

For individuals

  • Verify consequential AI claims with original sources.
  • Limit sensitive data shared with tools you do not control.
  • Retain a human decision point for money, health, rights and identity.

For organisations

  • Define permissions and stop conditions before deploying agents.
  • Log tool actions, retries and attempted boundary crossings.
  • Test safe interruption, escalation and revocation regularly.

When something goes wrong

  • Pause affected automations using authorised controls.
  • Preserve logs without exposing personal information.
  • Escalate to accountable human owners and appropriate incident responders.

Human agency lab

Before calling any AI system humanity-aligned, we should test observable behaviour rather than rely on promises.

Consent

Can users clearly understand and decline consequential actions?

Intervention

Does the system stop safely when its authority is revoked?

Accountability

Can decisions be inspected, contested and corrected?

This lab describes proposed tests; no independent assurance or certification is claimed.
