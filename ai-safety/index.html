Key developments and continuity watchpoints

Independent tests exposed historical safeguards gaps

US GAO tested four selected AI platforms in spring 2026 and found that each could yield harmful assistance under its assigned scenario. The agency released its audit publicly on 18 November. These observations do not establish a present-day failure rate, rank the platforms or document actual harm. Recheck safeguards after significant model updates.

Source-based investigative brief

A scoped review, not a comprehensive product audit

A controlled audit, a parliamentary recommendation, an open regulator inquiry and a volunteer survey answer different questions. We do not combine them into a safety score or infer that a policy proposal or installed fix is already effective. The common practical test is whether people can understand, stop and contest a consequential decision.

What counts as proof that a safeguard works?

The evidence is strongest when an independent reviewer can test a particular safeguard and identify what it did not prove.
