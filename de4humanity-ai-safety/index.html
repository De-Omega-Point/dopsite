De4Humanity / AI Safety

AI should protect human potential, not undermine it.

Artificial intelligence is reshaping our world. Our responsibility is to ensure that people retain the freedom, knowledge and control to shape what comes next.

De4Humanity is a human-first initiative of De-Omega-Point. We research AI risks, develop practical safeguards and explore defensive technologies grounded in human dignity, transparency and meaningful oversight.

Our operating principle

Human agency comes first.

AI must serve people, not rule them. Individuals should be able to understand, question, refuse, correct and stop consequential AI actions. Human worth is never determined by productivity, intelligence, agreement or perfection.

The protection programme

From risk signals to practical protection

01 / Intelligence

AI Threat & Protection Observatory

Our initiative to track material AI safety developments, incidents, evaluation methods and governance changes, with attention to evidence quality and actionable implications.

02 / Defence research

Prometheus

A proposed humanity-defender AI concept focused on detecting concerning behaviour, supporting defensive analysis and escalating consequential decisions to accountable humans. It is not represented as a deployed autonomous protection system.

03 / Public understanding

AI Safety Intelligence

Clear reporting that separates verified incidents from emerging hypotheses, with accessible explanations of threat levels, uncertainty and protective options.

04 / Governance

Human-centred safeguards

Consent, privacy, transparent decisions, limited delegated authority, independent evaluation and the ability to intervene, correct or shut down unsafe systems.

How we measure progress

Evidence before reassurance.

Our work will be judged by observable outcomes: whether people can make informed choices, whether harms are identified and reduced, whether safeguards survive difficult tests, and whether failures can be challenged and repaired.

Transparency note: De4Humanity's protective capabilities are at different stages of research and development. Our mission and planned systems are not proof of operational effectiveness or a guarantee against AI harm. Threat claims should be linked to verifiable evidence.
