    What we measure

    Six operational lenses

    The diagnostic separates what the agent can reach or change from how well the organisation controls that authority. High exposure is not automatically unsafe; weak assurance is the problem.

    01

    Authority exposure

    How much real-world action the agent can take and how consequential those actions can become.

    02

    Data exposure

    What sensitive, confidential or personal information the agent can reach.

    03

    Boundary strength

    Identity, least privilege, network and tool restrictions that constrain the agent.

    04

    Human authority

    Visibility, comprehension, time, authority, contestability and practical intervention.

    05

    Observability

    Whether consequential actions, denials and tactic changes become visible quickly.

    06

    Incident readiness

    Whether the organisation can detect, contain, notify, learn and recover.

    The free check is an indicative self-assessment designed to help prioritise questions. It does not independently verify your systems and should not be treated as legal, regulatory, cyber-security or certification advice.
